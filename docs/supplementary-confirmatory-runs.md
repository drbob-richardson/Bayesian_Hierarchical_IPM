# Supplementary Confirmatory Runs for the Identifiability Paper

Four additional runs requested for the confounding/identifiability manuscript
(`paper/methods_simulation_results.tex`). All use the existing positive
inverse-IPM recovery model and Laplace posterior approximation. Scripts are
under `scripts/`; outputs under `results/`.

## 1. Recruitment confounding, numerically confirmed

`scripts/run_confounding_likelihood_slice.R` →
`results/confounding_slice/`.

A single large, low-noise dataset (one 8-transition trajectory, full
detection, ~6000 fish) was generated from the inverse-IPM model at truth, so
the result reflects *structural* identifiability rather than Monte Carlo noise.

- Posterior correlation of recruitment level and slope
  (`log_recruitment_at_80`, `recruitment_slope_20`): **-0.96**.
- Curvature of the 2-D profile log-likelihood implies a ridge correlation of
  **0.98** in that plane.
- Observed-information condition number: **~7.1e3** (strong weak
  identification).
- The global least-informed direction at this design is dominated by the
  recruit-*size* combination (`recruit_mean_80`, `recruit_mean_slope_20`),
  distinct from the recruitment-*intensity* ridge — both are real and worth
  separating in the text.

This reproduces the manuscript's ~-0.99 level/slope correlation (which arose
under shorter series) and supplies the figure/derivation target for the
confounding proposition.

## 2. Simulation-based calibration of the Laplace posterior

`scripts/run_sbc.R` → `results/sbc/informative_gamma_poisson/`.

200 SBC iterations; data generated from the recovery model's own
data-generating process (NB profile counts), so rank departures isolate
computational/approximation error rather than misspecification. 99 posterior
draws per iteration; ranks in 0..99 (expected mean 49.5).

- **200/200 valid fits, 0 failures.**
- 10 of 11 parameters show uniform ranks (chi-square p > 0.10).
- Only `log_process_precision` is mildly miscalibrated (mean rank 44.5,
  p = 0.015) — expected for an overdispersion parameter.

Conclusion: the Laplace approximation the paper relies on is well calibrated
for the demographic coefficients; the process-precision nuisance is slightly
under-dispersed and should be flagged.

### Weak-prior SBC (diffuse regime)

`SBC_REGIME=weak` → `results/sbc/weak_gamma_poisson/`. 198/200 valid fits
(2 failures: one degenerate projection, one with no finite Laplace mode).
Under the diffuse prior the ranks are **strongly non-uniform** (chi-square
p down to 8e-30). Mean ranks stay near-central (~45–54), so this is a
*dispersion* failure, not a location bias: the rank histograms are
**U-shaped**, with extreme-bin frequency ~7–9× nominal (e.g.
`recruitment_slope_20` 0.177, `log_recruitment_at_80` 0.157,
`growth_increment_80`/`growth_slope_20` 0.152, vs. expected 0.02). The
Laplace intervals are **overconfident**, and the truth falls in the tails far
too often.

Crucially, the worst-calibrated parameters are exactly the confounded
directions identified in Run 1 (recruitment level/slope and growth), tying the
SBC overconfidence to the structural ridge. Two distinct causes are conflated
here — genuine Gaussian-approximation inadequacy where simulated populations
are extreme/boundary, and the wide prior placing mass on degenerate biology —
so weak-prior SBC should be read as a caution rather than a pure computation
test. Practical implication: under diffuse priors, report intervals from full
MCMC rather than Laplace, or use a realistic prior. This is consistent with the
manuscript's repeated "biased and overconfident" observation along confounded
directions, now quantified by calibration.

## 3. Observation-model robustness

`scripts/run_detection_robustness.R` → `results/detection_robustness/`.

The recovery model treats the profile as a constant multiple of latent
abundance; a constant, size-independent detection is absorbed by the linear
projection. This run stresses that with 20 replicates each of constant,
size-dependent, and unknown time-varying-effort detection, data generated by
the individual-based simulator. Mean 95% coverage:

| Detection | Param. coverage | Survival@80 | Recruit@80 | λ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| constant (Gamma–Poisson) | 0.83 | 0.95 | 0.50 | 0.50 |
| size-dependent (Gamma–Poisson) | **0.59** | **0.00** | 0.10 | 0.75 |
| time-varying effort (Gamma–Poisson) | 0.81 | 0.65 | 0.55 | 0.45 |
| constant (Poisson) | 0.84 | 0.90 | 0.55 | 0.80 |
| size-dependent (Poisson) | **0.60** | **0.00** | 0.10 | 0.65 |
| time-varying effort (Poisson) | 0.76 | 0.65 | 0.30 | 0.35 |

- **Size-dependent detection is the dangerous failure:** survival-at-80
  coverage collapses to 0 because changing profile shape from detection is
  absorbed as demographic change. This quantifies the manuscript's
  observation-model warning.
- Time-varying effort degrades survival coverage moderately (0.95 → 0.65).
- One Poisson constant-detection replicate produced an explosive λ estimate
  (mean abs λ error 7.33 vs 0.025 for Gamma–Poisson), consistent with the
  paper's finding that independent overdispersion stabilizes recovery.

## 4. Higher-replicate transportability boundary

`scripts/run_transportability_boundary.R` (now parameterized by
`TRANSPORT_REPLICATES`, `TRANSPORT_DIR`, `TRANSPORT_CORES`) with 30 replicates
→ `results/transportability_boundary_highrep/`. 1170 fits, all converged.

The draft's transportability boundary used only 9–10 paired datasets per point
and was labeled exploratory. At 30 replicates (~87–89 datasets per pooled
cell) the qualitative findings hold and tighten:

- Full borrowing: coverage-at-80 collapses 0.93 → 0.64 → 0.12 → 0 as bias
  rises through 0.25, 0.50, 0.75; RMSE ratio to profiles-only stays well below
  1 throughout.
- Robust borrowing: coverage degrades gracefully (0.85 → 0.66 → 0.42 → 0.37
  → 0.45 → 0.53) with less point-estimate gain; mean posterior external-study
  weight declines 0.75 → 0.46 with bias.
- Largest tested safe bias: 0.25 (baseline, low-survival) and 0 (flatter
  fecundity) under both borrowing rules — slightly more tolerant than the
  10-replicate run, which read 0 throughout.

## Reproduction

```sh
Rscript scripts/run_confounding_likelihood_slice.R
SBC_ITERATIONS=200 SBC_CORES=12 Rscript scripts/run_sbc.R
DETECTION_REPLICATES=20 DETECTION_CORES=8 Rscript scripts/run_detection_robustness.R
TRANSPORT_REPLICATES=30 TRANSPORT_DIR=results/transportability_boundary_highrep \
  TRANSPORT_CORES=14 Rscript scripts/run_transportability_boundary.R
```
